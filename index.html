04 / BUILT
Term Project · Trimester 7

Spacecraft Telemetry Anomaly Detection

Complete

A comparative study of statistical and time-series methods for catching faults in spacecraft subsystem telemetry before they become failures. Built on the Skoltech Anomaly Benchmark (SKAB) dataset — 44,534 sensor readings across 33 experiments, with ground-truth labels for valve failures, rotor imbalance, and cavitation faults.

Two detectors were built and compared head-to-head: a Z-score threshold detector using rolling mean/std, and an ARIMA residual detector that models expected sensor behavior and flags deviations. Z-score came out near-perfect on recall (0.9998) but ARIMA was the sharper instrument on precision (0.4838) — the two methods turned out to be complementary, one built for sensitivity, the other for specificity. Results are explorable through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

Astronomy · AI Pipeline

ChhayaPath

छाया पथ — "the shadow's path"

An AI pipeline that finds exoplanet transit signals buried in noisy light curves, then tells them apart from look-alikes — eclipsing binaries, stellar blends, starspots. Built on TESS data, it classifies each dip and estimates the transit's period, depth, and duration, with a confidence score attached to every detection.
